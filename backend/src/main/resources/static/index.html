Blood Compatibility Guide

Safe transfusion matching rules for donors and recipients

Full Donor → Recipient Matrix

Key Clinical Rules

O- is the Universal Donor: Red blood cells from O- can be safely transfused to any blood group in emergencies.
AB+ is the Universal Recipient: Patients with AB+ can receive red blood cells from all 8 blood groups.
Rh Factor Rule: Rh-negative patients should strictly receive Rh-negative blood to prevent antibodies.
